COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently asked questions

Can I deposit money into Northstar?

No. This demonstration does not accept deposits or process payments.

Can I withdraw the sample cash balance?

No. The displayed figures are fictional and have no cash value.

Is my demo dashboard private?

No. This is a static website, and the demo pages are publicly accessible. Do not treat the dashboard as a private account or enter sensitive information.

Does submitting a message contact Northstar?

There is currently no connected support-ticket system or contact form. This page only provides information.

Will real investment services be added?

Any future real-money service would require appropriate legal and regulatory review, verified providers, secure infrastructure, and properly tested integrations before it could be offered.
