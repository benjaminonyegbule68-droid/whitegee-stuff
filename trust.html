RISK DISCLOSURE

Investment risk matters

Real investments can fall in value, and investors can lose some or all of their capital. Digital assets can be especially volatile. Liquidity, counterparty, custody, operational, fraud, and regulatory risks may also apply.

The sample balances shown by Northstar are not live market prices or real investments. They should not be used to make financial decisions.

BEFORE USING A REAL PROVIDER

What you should verify

  • The operator's legal name and registered business details.
  • Applicable licences and authorisations through the relevant regulator.
  • Who holds customer funds and assets, and under what legal arrangements.
  • All fees, withdrawal conditions, and product risks.
  • Whether financial statements and performance claims can be independently verified.
  • How complaints, data access, and security incidents are handled.
