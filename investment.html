UNDERSTAND THE RISKS

What to consider

Market risk

Asset prices can rise or fall. You could lose some or all of the money invested in a real investment.

Fees and costs

Real investment services should disclose applicable charges, spreads, management fees, and other costs before a customer commits funds.

Liquidity

The ability to sell an asset and receive proceeds depends on the asset, market conditions, provider rules, and settlement arrangements.

Verification

Before using a real investment provider, independently verify its legal identity, regulatory status, custody arrangements, and applicable investor protections.
